# Rainicorn Sky Dash

A 2D arcade platformer where you play as a rainbow-maned unicorn racing against a 15-second timer across a starry night sky.

## How to Play

- **W / ↑** — Jump
- **A / ←** — Move left
- **D / →** — Move right
- **Mouse Click** — Shoot darkness platforms

**Goal:** Shoot dark platforms to convert them into solid rainbow platforms. Each conversion earns **+10 points** and **+1.5 seconds** of bonus time. Rainbow platforms are temporary and fade after 4 seconds. Standing on the ground drains time 4× faster — stay airborne!
